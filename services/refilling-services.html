Our Service Workflow

Professional support built around reliability.

Refilling of all types of Fire Extinguishers with original quality materials as per safety standards.

Activities are planned around equipment, site conditions and applicable requirements. Where relevant, service work can follow applicable Indian IS requirements and other applicable national and international standards.

01 — Inspect the cylinder, valve and overall condition
02 — Safely discharge residual pressure
03 — Clean and test the tank/cylinder
04 — Refill with the correct agent to exact weight specifications
05 — Pressurize with nitrogen to the required pressure
06 — Apply a new tamper seal and service tag
07 — Final verification and service documentation
